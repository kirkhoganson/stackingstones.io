Our research tool

Our internal research tool helps us decide what to make next. It connects to our own Google Ads account to read keyword ideas and search-volume estimates, and combines them with public marketplace data to compare topics. The tool is used only by Stacking Stones, for our own products. It does not run ads, manage other people's accounts, or access anyone else's data.
