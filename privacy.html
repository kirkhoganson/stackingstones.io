Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains how Stacking Stones LLC ("Stacking Stones", "we") handles information on stackingstones.io and in Stacking Stones, our internal research tool.

This website

This website collects no personal information. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and no forms. Its fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our research tool and Google user data

Stacking Stones, our internal research tool, signs in with Google to access one Google Ads account: our own. It is used only by Stacking Stones and is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: keyword ideas and search-volume estimates from the Google Ads API for our own account. It does not access email, contacts, files, or any other Google data, and it does not access anyone else's account.
  • How it is used: only to research topics for our own products. It is not used to run ads, to serve ads for others, or to build profiles of people.
  • How it is stored: on our own computers, protected by our device and account security. Sign-in credentials are kept in the operating system's secure keychain.
  • How it is shared: it is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone, and it is not transferred to third parties.
  • Retention: we keep research data for as long as it is useful for our product decisions and delete it when it is not.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access granted to the tool can be removed at any time from the third-party access page of the Google Account that granted it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
